Head of Engineering at Fans Holdings

I lead the team and the platform behind FansAPI.

I’m Diego, Head of Engineering at Fans Holdings in Prague. FansAPI (formerly OnlyFansAPI) is our API platform for businesses in the creator economy.

I lead architecture decisions and our monitoring tooling, I’m on call when something goes down, and I’m the direct technical contact for our enterprise customers. I also own technical hiring: I review applicants’ test tasks and run the final interviews.

I’m still hands-on, with around 1,650 commits to the core platform since April 2025. Before this I built loyalty software at Piggy / Leat, worked at Dutch agencies, and ran technology for the non-profit Awoostria.
